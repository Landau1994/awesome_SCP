# CPTAC

**Clinical Proteomic Tumor Analysis Consortium — an NCI program that performs comprehensive proteogenomic characterization of major cancer types, providing paired genomic, transcriptomic, and deep proteomic/phosphoproteomic data on TCGA-characterized tumors and beyond.**

## Overview

CPTAC extends [[TCGA]]'s genomic analyses with deep mass-spectrometry-based proteomics and phosphoproteomics. By generating quantitative protein-level data alongside matched genomic and transcriptomic profiles, CPTAC enables the study of how genomic alterations translate into functional protein changes in cancer. The data is publicly available through the Proteomic Data Commons (PDC).

## Statistics

CPTAC is a research program, not a single uniform dataset. Its studies cover multiple human cancer types and include proteomics, phosphoproteomics and matched genomic/transcriptomic measurements. Sample numbers, tumor/normal matching, assay types and access conditions vary by cohort; consult the study record in the Proteomic Data Commons (PDC).

## Application in SCP

Paired bulk data from CPTAC can support RNA–protein modeling and comparison with single-cell results. scTranslator reports a multi-source bulk corpus; its aggregate sample count must not be attributed to CPTAC alone. Cohort-level contributions and pairing should be checked against the original study supplement.

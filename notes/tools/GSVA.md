# GSVA

## Overview

GSVA is an R/Bioconductor package for estimating gene-set enrichment variation across samples. It transforms a gene-by-sample expression matrix and gene sets into set-level scores for downstream comparisons.

## Conditions and Limitations

Select the method and expression-distribution settings appropriate to the data. Gene-set scores summarize coordinated expression and are not direct measurements of pathway activity; proteomics requires identifier mapping and handling of missing data.

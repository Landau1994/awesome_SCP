# RETICLE

## Overview

RETICLE is a real-time-search-assisted acquisition strategy on a Tribrid mass spectrometer for multiplexed single-cell proteomics. Rapid ion-trap identification helps prioritize precursors for Orbitrap quantification; it is not a general downstream quantification package.

RETICLE is a workflow/tool label used in single-cell proteomics acquisition-analysis pipelines.

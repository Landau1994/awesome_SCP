# Orbitrap

## Overview

Orbitrap refers to an electrostatic mass analyzer and the family of mass spectrometers incorporating it. These instruments support high-resolution, accurate-mass measurements in proteomics and other applications.

## Conditions and Limitations

Acquisition speed, sensitivity, resolving power, fragmentation and other analyzers differ among models. Orbitrap is not synonymous with [[Orbitrap Astral]], and the Stellar instrument has a different analyzer architecture.

# omicsGMF

## Overview

omicsGMF provides generalized matrix-factorization methods for omics data, with applications to dimensionality reduction, visualization and missing-value estimation through the sgdGMF framework.

## Conditions and Limitations

Choose the observation model and covariates to match the measurements. A fitted or imputed value is model-derived and should not be treated as an independently measured protein abundance.

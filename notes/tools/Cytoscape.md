# Cytoscape

## Overview

Cytoscape is open-source software for visualizing and analyzing networks and integrating attribute data such as expression measurements. Apps extend its functionality, including [[ClueGO]] for functional annotation networks.

## Conditions and Limitations

Interpret edges according to their source (interaction, correlation or inference). A visualized network alone does not establish causality or validate a biological interaction.

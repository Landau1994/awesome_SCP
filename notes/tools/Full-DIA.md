# Full-DIA

## Overview

Full-DIA is a deep-learning-based DIA analysis framework that constructs a missing-value-free selected protein quantification matrix from diaPASEF data under its global FDR procedure. “Complete” refers to the reported matrix and workflow conditions, not measurement of every protein in a cell.

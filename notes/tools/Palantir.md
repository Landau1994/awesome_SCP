# Palantir

## Overview

Palantir infers differentiation trajectories and cell-fate probabilities from single-cell data using a probabilistic model. It produces pseudotime, differentiation potential and branch probabilities.

## Conditions and Limitations

Results depend on preprocessing, starting-cell selection and manifold coverage. Pseudotime is an inferred ordering, not a measured clock or direct proof of lineage transitions.

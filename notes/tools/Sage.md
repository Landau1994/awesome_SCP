# Sage

## Overview

Sage is a proteomics database-search engine for peptide identification from tandem mass spectra, with quantitative workflows described in its documentation. This entry refers to the proteomics project by Michael Lazear.

## Conditions and Limitations

Choose sequence databases, enzyme/modification settings, tolerances and FDR handling for the acquisition. Performance depends on the release, search space, data and hardware.

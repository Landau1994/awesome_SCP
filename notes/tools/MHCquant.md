# MHCquant

## Overview

nf-core/mhcquant is a Nextflow-based analysis pipeline for immunopeptidomics, supporting identification and quantification of MHC-associated peptides from mass-spectrometry data.

## Conditions and Limitations

Provide the required samplesheet, compatible spectra and sequence database for the chosen release. Peptide identification and binding prediction do not alone demonstrate antigen immunogenicity.

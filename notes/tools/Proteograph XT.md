# Proteograph XT

## Overview

Proteograph XT is a Seer sample-preparation assay/workflow using nanoparticle protein-corona enrichment and automated processing to produce peptides for LC-MS. It is part of the Proteograph product suite, not a spectrum-search engine.

## Conditions and Limitations

Sample type, assay-kit configuration, instrument and downstream analysis determine coverage. Vendor cohort-level identification counts are not per-sample or single-cell performance guarantees.

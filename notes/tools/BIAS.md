# BIAS

## Overview

BIAS (Biology Image Analysis Software) is used in Deep Visual Proteomics for image analysis and selection of cellular objects for downstream laser microdissection and proteomics. Its role is image-based segmentation/classification and selection, rather than peptide-spectrum identification.

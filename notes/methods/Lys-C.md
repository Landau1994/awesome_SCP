# Lys-C

## Overview

Lys-C (endoproteinase Lys-C) is a protease that cleaves peptide bonds C-terminal to lysine. Dual Lys-C/[[Trypsin]] digestion improves digestion efficiency and sequence coverage, and is used in low-input and single-cell protocols (e.g., the HUPO inter-laboratory SCP benchmarking study).

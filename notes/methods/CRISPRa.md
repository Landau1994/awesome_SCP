# CRISPRa

## Overview

CRISPR activation (CRISPRa) uses guide RNAs and a catalytically inactive Cas protein coupled to transcriptional activators to increase expression of endogenous genes. It is a gene-regulation perturbation, not a DNA-cutting knockout.

In proteomics and surfaceome screens, CRISPRa can connect increased gene expression to protein abundance or cell phenotypes. Activation efficiency depends on guide position, the target locus and cell state; protein-level changes require measurement rather than being inferred solely from RNA.

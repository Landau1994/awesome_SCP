# Proximity Labeling

## Overview

Proximity labeling (PL) uses an enzyme or photocatalyst fused to a bait molecule to covalently tag proteins within nanometer range, followed by enrichment and MS identification — a core strategy for mapping the cell surface proteome and interactomes. Major systems: [[APEX]]/[[APEX2]] (peroxidase), [[BioID]]/[[TurboID]] (biotin ligase), and photocatalytic platforms such as [[μMap]]; related approaches include miniSOG, EMARS, and PUP-IT.

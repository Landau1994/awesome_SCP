# μMap

## Overview

μMap is a photocatalytic proximity-labeling platform: a photocatalyst conjugated to an antibody or ligand activates diazirine-biotin reagents with visible light, tagging proteins within nanometer distance of the target. Used for mapping cell-surface protein neighborhoods and ligand receptors.

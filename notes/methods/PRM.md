# PRM

## Overview

Parallel reaction monitoring (PRM) isolates a targeted precursor and measures many of its fragment ions together with a high-resolution, accurate-mass analyzer. Fragment chromatograms can then be selected for peptide identification and quantification.

Unlike conventional [[MRM]], PRM records a full fragment spectrum for each selected target rather than only preselected fragment transitions. It still requires validated targets, retention-time scheduling where appropriate, and interference and false-identification checks.

# Isobaric Labeling

## Overview

Isobaric labeling (TMT, iTRAQ) multiplexes samples with mass tags of identical nominal mass that fragment into reporter ions for relative quantification. In single-cell proteomics, a high-abundance carrier channel boosts peptide detection while single-cell channels are quantified via reporter ions — the core of [[SCoPE2]]-type workflows.

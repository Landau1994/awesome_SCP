# RNA-Seq

## Overview

RNA sequencing measures transcript identities and relative abundance by sequencing RNA-derived libraries. Conventional workflows reverse-transcribe selected RNA into cDNA, sequence the library, and assign reads to genes or transcripts. Bulk, single-cell and single-nucleus assays have different sampling and normalization requirements.

RNA abundance does not directly measure protein abundance. Library preparation, transcript length, sequencing depth and biological replication influence interpretation; gene-level and isoform-level analyses answer different questions.

# snRNA-seq

## Overview

Single-nucleus RNA sequencing profiles RNA from isolated nuclei rather than intact cells. It can be applied to frozen or difficult-to-dissociate tissues, including archived brain samples, and provides a nuclear transcriptome readout rather than a complete cellular RNA measurement.

Nuclear RNA contains substantial unspliced transcripts. Counting intronic reads can improve sensitivity, but the reference, counting settings and comparison with whole-cell RNA-seq must be documented.

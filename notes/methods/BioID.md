# BioID

## Overview

BioID uses a promiscuous biotin ligase (BirA*) fused to a bait protein to biotinylate interacting or nearby proteins over hours, which are then enriched on streptavidin and identified by MS. Its faster derivative [[TurboID]] shortens labeling to minutes.

# APEX2

## Overview

APEX2 is an engineered ascorbate peroxidase variant with improved catalytic efficiency and cellular labeling sensitivity compared with the original [[APEX]]. It is widely used for spatially resolved proteome mapping, including mitochondrial and cell-surface proximity labeling.

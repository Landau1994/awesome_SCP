# nanoflow dual-trap single-column liquid chromatography

## Overview

nanoDTSC uses two trap columns feeding one analytical column. Sample loading and cleanup on one trap overlap with analysis from the other, reducing idle time between injections.

## Reported Benchmark

Kreimer et al. (2023) reported a 15-minute total run per cell, including 11.5 minutes of peptide quantification, enabling 96 single-cell runs per day. In their cardiomyocyte and aorta-cell experiments, the workflow quantified more than 1,000 proteins per cell. These values refer to the evaluated chromatography, sample preparation and MS settings.

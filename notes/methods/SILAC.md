# SILAC

## Overview

SILAC (stable isotope labeling by amino acids in cell culture) metabolically incorporates heavy isotope-labeled amino acids into proteins, enabling accurate relative quantification by LC-MS. Pulsed SILAC ([[pSILAC]]) tracks newly synthesized protein in cell populations; [[SC-pSILAC]] adapts this principle to single-cell measurements.

# nano-DESI

## Overview

Nanospray desorption electrospray ionization (nano-DESI) extracts analytes from a surface through a small liquid junction and transfers the dissolved material to an electrospray emitter for mass spectrometry. It is an ambient liquid-extraction ionization method.

Spatial resolution and analyte coverage depend on probe geometry, liquid junction, solvent and instrument settings. Protein-oriented adaptations can support intact-proteoform sampling, but the original technique is not limited to proteins or single cells.

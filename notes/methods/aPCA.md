# aPCA

## Overview

In the Human Domainome study, aPCA means **abundance protein fragment complementation assay**. A protein domain is fused to a fragment of dihydrofolate reductase (DHFR); complementation couples its cellular abundance to growth under selection. Sequencing variant frequencies before and after selection estimates variant effects on abundance. This assay is distinct from principal component analysis (PCA).

## Interpretation

- Reduced abundance can indicate destabilization and degradation, but the assay measures a cellular abundance phenotype rather than a direct thermodynamic folding energy.
- Domain context, expression and assay dynamic range affect interpretation.

# APEX

## Overview

APEX (ascorbate peroxidase) is a proximity-labeling enzyme that generates short-lived radicals from biotin-phenol substrates in the presence of hydrogen peroxide, biotinylating nearby proteins within seconds to minutes. Its engineered variant [[APEX2]] offers improved activity and stability.

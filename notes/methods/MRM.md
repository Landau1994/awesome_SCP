# MRM

## Overview

Multiple reaction monitoring (MRM), also commonly called selected reaction monitoring (SRM), is a targeted MS/MS approach that monitors predefined precursor-to-fragment ion transitions, usually on a triple-quadrupole instrument. Multiple transitions and retention-time agreement help identify and quantify a target peptide.

Assays require transition selection, interference assessment and suitable standards. The number of monitored targets and dwell time affect cycle time and sensitivity; MRM does not measure an unrestricted proteome.

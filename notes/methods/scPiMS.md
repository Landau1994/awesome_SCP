# scPiMS

## Overview

scPiMS is a top-down platform for proteoform-level profiling of endogenous single cells, combining low-input sample preparation with individual-ion mass spectrometry on Orbitrap instruments. Demonstrated on dissociated rat hippocampal cells, including neurons and glial cell classes.

> [!cite] Primary Reference
> Su P, Hollas MAR, Pla I, et al. *Nature Biotechnology* 44, 563–567 (2026 issue; published online 15 May 2025).
> [10.1038/s41587-025-02669-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-025-02669-x)

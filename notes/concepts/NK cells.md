# NK cells

## Overview

Natural killer (NK) cells are cytotoxic lymphocytes of the innate immune system. Their activity reflects activating and inhibitory signals. The linked surfaceome study examines how particular tumor-cell surface proteins affect NK-cell killing; those findings depend on the tested cells and experimental setting.

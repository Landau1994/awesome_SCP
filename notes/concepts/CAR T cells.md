# CAR T cells

## Overview

CAR T cells are T cells engineered to express chimeric antigen receptors that recognize defined cell-surface antigens and redirect T-cell activity. CD19 is a common target in established cancer applications. In this vault they provide a context for tumor-antigen discovery and surfaceome research.

# Proteoform

## Overview

A proteoform is a specific molecular form of the protein product of a gene, arising from genetic sequence variation, alternative RNA processing and/or post-translational modification. The term was proposed by Smith and Kelleher (2013); it underpins [[Top-down proteomics|top-down proteomics]] and proteoform characterization.

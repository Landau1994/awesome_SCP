# MBP

## Summary

MBP encodes myelin basic protein. Classical MBP isoforms are major structural constituents of central nervous system myelin, while other isoforms have broader developmental and cellular roles. In this vault MBP refers to myelin basic protein, not eosinophil major basic protein or bacterial maltose-binding protein.

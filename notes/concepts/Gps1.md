# Gps1

## Summary

In the mammalian embryo study linked in this vault, mouse Gps1 encodes G protein pathway suppressor 1, also called COP9 signalosome subunit 1 (CSN1). It should not be interpreted as the similarly named budding-yeast polarity protein.

## Notes

- Interpret the gene identifier and species together.
- Membership in the COP9 signalosome is distinct from yeast Gps1 biology.

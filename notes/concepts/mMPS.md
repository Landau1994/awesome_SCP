# mMPS

## Summary

In the linked protein-domain study, mMPS means microchip-based massive in parallel synthesis. It is a high-throughput DNA/oligonucleotide synthesis technology used to construct variant libraries encoding protein domains, not direct synthesis of the proteins themselves.

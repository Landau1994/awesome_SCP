# CD43

## Overview

CD43 (sialophorin/leukosialin; gene SPN) is a heavily glycosylated transmembrane protein found on leukocytes. It participates in T-cell activation, migration and cell interactions. Ligand activity depends on the glycosylation and cellular context; the linked surfaceome study discusses its relationship to NK-cell killing.

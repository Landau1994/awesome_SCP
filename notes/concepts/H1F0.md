# H1F0

## Summary

H1F0 is a historical symbol for the human H1-0 gene, which encodes the linker histone H1.0. H1.0 binds linker DNA and is associated with chromatin organization and terminal differentiation or low proliferation.

## Notes

- Frequently discussed in cell-state maturation and chromatin compaction contexts.
- Can serve as a marker in differentiation-associated analyses.

## References

- Histone-related regulatory marker.

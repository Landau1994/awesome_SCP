# PDPN

## Overview

PDPN (podoplanin) is a small, heavily O-glycosylated transmembrane protein expressed on lymphatic endothelial cells, stromal fibroblasts, and some tumors. It activates platelets through CLEC-2 and is linked to tumor invasion and metastasis.

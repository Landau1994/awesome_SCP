# CD44

## Overview

CD44 is a cell-surface glycoprotein receptor for hyaluronan that participates in adhesion, migration and signaling. Its expression and splice variants can mark particular cell states, but CD44 expression alone does not establish a cancer stem-cell identity or metastatic behavior.

# hi12

## Summary

hi12 is a human induced pluripotent stem-cell line derived from human foreskin fibroblasts and used in the linked SC-pSILAC study. It is a specific cell-line identity, not a generic abbreviation for iPSCs. The 2021 source describes hFF ATCC CRL-2429; the later study links hi12 to an hFF-1 entry. These catalogue descriptions should not be assumed interchangeable without checking the cell-line provenance.

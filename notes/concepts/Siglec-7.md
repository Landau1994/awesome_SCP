# Siglec-7

## Overview

Siglec-7 (CD328) is a sialic-acid-binding inhibitory receptor expressed on NK cells. Engagement by appropriate sialylated ligands can inhibit NK-cell cytotoxicity. Binding depends on the glycan structure and presentation; a single glycosidic linkage is not a sufficient definition of ligand specificity.

# Siglec-1

## Overview

Siglec-1 (CD169, sialoadhesin) is a sialic-acid-binding adhesion receptor associated mainly with macrophages. It mediates interactions with sialylated cells and particles. Unlike inhibitory Siglecs such as [[Siglec-7]], it should not be described as the same NK-cell inhibitory checkpoint.
